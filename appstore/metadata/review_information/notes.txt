This app shows Korean Lotto 6/45 winning numbers (public data published by the official operator), statistics, a number generator for entertainment, and a QR code scanner that checks a printed ticket against the published results.
The app does not sell lottery tickets, take bets, or process any payments, and contains no real-money gaming. It is an unofficial informational app and is not affiliated with the lottery operator.
The camera is used only to scan the QR code printed on the user's own lottery ticket (QR 당첨확인 tab). No login is required.
